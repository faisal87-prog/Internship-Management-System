# User Journey

Complete numbered MVP flow:

1. Admin creates Mentor accounts.
2. Admin creates Intern accounts.
3. Admin assigns interns to a Mentor.
4. Mentor creates an internship program.
5. Mentor enters program details.
6. Mentor chooses roadmap scope: Entire Program, Selected Interns, or Individual Intern.
7. Mentor requests AI roadmap generation.
8. AI generates the roadmap as a draft.
9. Mentor reviews and edits the roadmap.
10. Mentor publishes the roadmap.
11. Tasks for the relevant week appear for assigned interns.
12. Interns work on assigned tasks.
13. Interns update individual task status.
14. Interns submit written responses or files.
15. Interns may submit multiple versions.
16. Mentor reviews each intern's submission individually.
17. Mentor provides feedback.
18. Mentor assigns a score out of 100.
19. AI analyses weekly performance data.
20. AI generates a weekly report as a draft.
21. Mentor reviews, edits, regenerates, or approves the report.
22. Approved reports become visible to the intern.
23. The weekly process repeats until the internship ends.
24. AI generates the final internship summary.
25. Mentor reviews and edits the summary.
26. Mentor adds final score or comments.
27. Mentor approves the final summary.
28. The final summary can be downloaded as PDF.
29. Admin views an overview of mentors, interns, programs, tasks, submissions, weekly reports, and final summaries.
30. The intern account is deactivated after program completion.
31. Admin may reactivate the intern account later.
